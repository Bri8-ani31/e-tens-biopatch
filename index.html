Autonomous, Drug-Free Relief for Period Pain

The world’s first closed-loop biometric wearable targeting T10–L1 spinal segments. Powered by ESP32, real-time EHG/GSR sensing, and synchronized thermal vasodilation.

Watch Video Demo Source Code

The Problem

Over 80% of women suffer from primary dysmenorrhea. Conventional options like NSAIDs cause gastrointestinal risks, while standard TENS devices are generic, static, and manual.

Closed-Loop Sensing

Integrated EHG and GSR analog front-ends continuously monitor physiological stress markers to detect cramp onset before peak pain occurs.

Dual-Modality Therapy

Combines asymmetric biphasic stimulation (80–120 Hz) with dynamic thermal hysteresis control (25°C–50°C) to block spinal pain signals instantly.

Hardware & Firmware Architecture

Embedded Processing

  • Microcontroller: ESP32 Dual-Core (240MHz)
  • Display: 1.3" SH1106 OLED via I2C (0x3C)
  • Thermal Control: Solid-State Relay with Safety Hysteresis

Biometric Acquisition

  • Analog Processing: ADC1 Pins (GPIO 32, 33, 34)
  • Multi-Sensor: Pulse, ECG/EHG, and Galvanic Skin Response
  • Target BOM Cost: ₹800 – ₹1,200 per unit
